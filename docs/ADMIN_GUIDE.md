# Johanna's guide to the website

Everything on the site is managed from one place — **the admin**. No coding, ever.
Open `https://your-domain/admin/` and log in.

> The admin is a separate area for you only. Visitors never see it.

---

## The 5-minute tour

The left menu has everything:

| Menu item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Pages** | Every page of the site, as a tree. Gallery, blog, About… |
| **Images** | All your uploaded photos |
| **Shop** | **Orders** and **Shipping prices** |
| **Messages & commissions** | Everything visitors send you |
| **Settings** | Shop settings (PayPal…) and Site settings (footer, social links) |

Your **dashboard** (the first screen) shows what needs attention: orders waiting for
payment, orders to ship, new messages, open commissions, and what's running low.
Yellow ⚠️ boxes there tell you about things still to finish before launch.

## Add an artwork (the main thing you'll do)

1. **Pages → Gallery → "Add child page" → Artwork page.**
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — e.g. *Prague Rooftops*
   - **Main image** — upload the photo/scan (big files are fine, the site shrinks them)
   - **Short description** — one sentence for the gallery cards
   - **Story** — the memory behind the piece (shown in a nice card)
   - **Category, place, size, year, medium**
   - **Featured** — tick to show it on the homepage
3. **Ways to buy** — click *Add* for each thing you sell:
   - **Original painting** — set the price. It's one-of-a-kind, so once someone orders it,
     it becomes *Reserved*, then *Sold* when you mark the order paid.
   - **Print** — one row per size/paper (e.g. *A4 · 250g cotton paper*). Set a price and
     **stock** (how many you have). Leave stock **empty** if you print on demand.
   - **Digital download** — upload the high-resolution file. Customers get it only
     after you confirm payment.
   - Untick **Active** to hide an option without deleting it.
4. **Publish** (the green button at the bottom).

**Tip:** you can *Save draft* and publish later. Visitors only see published pages.

### Hide the price, or show the sketch
- **Gallery settings → "show price and allow buying"**: untick it and the price disappears
  everywhere. Visitors see *Price on request* and a button to message you instead.
  Buying is switched off while it is unticked. Tick it again any time.
- **Sketch image** (in *Artwork story*): add a photo of your pencil sketch or first wash,
  taken from the same angle. Visitors get a slider to drag from sketch to finished painting.
- The **colour dots** under each painting are found automatically — nothing to do.

### Extra images
Use *Extra images / process images* to add detail shots or work-in-progress photos.

## English and German

Every page exists once in English and once in German. Visitors switch with the
**EN / DE** buttons at the top.

To translate a page: **Pages → (the page) → "…" menu → Translate this page → German**.
Write the German text and publish. If a page has no German version yet, visitors who
click *DE* are simply taken to the German homepage.

> **Prices, stock and the sold/available status are managed on the English page only.**
> The German page shows them automatically, so the two can never disagree.

## Orders — what to do when someone buys

The customer gets an email with the amount, your PayPal/bank details and their order
number. **You** get an email too.

1. **Wait for the money** to arrive (PayPal or bank).
2. Open **Shop → Orders**, click the order, and set **Status → Paid**. Save.
   - The customer is emailed automatically.
   - Stock goes down, the "sold" counter goes up, and an original becomes *Sold*.
   - **Digital downloads** unlock instantly for the customer. A digital-only order is
     finished automatically.
3. For physical items: pack them, then set **Status → Shipped** and add the
   **carrier** and **tracking number**. The customer is emailed with tracking.
4. Status **Cancelled** puts everything back in stock (use it for unpaid orders
   or refunds). A cancelled order can't be reopened — the customer places a new one.

Statuses in order: *Awaiting payment → Paid → Being prepared → Shipped → Completed.*
The customer sees a progress bar on their own order page.

## Commissions & custom prints

Visitors fill in the contact form (with an optional reference photo). It appears under
**Messages & commissions**, and you get an email.

For a commission or custom-size print request:

1. Open it and look at the message and reference photo.
2. Enter your **Quote (€)** and a personal note, set **Status → Quote sent**.
   The customer gets your quote **and your payment details** by email.
3. When they've paid, tick **Payment received** and set **Accepted** → **In progress**
   → **Finished** → **Shipped** (add tracking). They get emails at the important steps
   and can follow progress on a private page.
4. **Declined** sends a polite "no thanks".

*New*, *Replied*, *Accepted* and *Closed* don't send emails — they're for your own tracking.

## Pages, menu, blog

- **Edit any text:** Pages → click the page → change → Publish. The homepage has
  fields for every section.
- **Menu:** a page appears in the top menu when **"Show in menus"** is ticked
  (Promote tab). Drag pages in the tree to change the order.
- **New page** (About, FAQ, …): Pages → Home → *Add child page* → **Standard page**.
  Build it from blocks: text, images, quotes, buttons, price lists, FAQs, numbered steps.
- **Blog post:** Pages → Blog → *Add child page* → **Blog post**. Add a cover image,
  an intro, tags, and the text. You can link a post to an artwork.
- **Footer links:** on a Standard page, tick **"Show in footer"** (Promote tab).

## Settings you should set before launch

**Settings → Shop settings**
- **Order notification email** — where alerts go.
- **PayPal email** and **PayPal.me name** (the part after `paypal.me/`). With the name set,
  customers get a button with the exact amount filled in.
- **IBAN** etc. for bank transfers (clear the IBAN to hide that option).
- **Free shipping in Germany from** — optional.
- **Small business (§19 UStG)** — tick if you don't charge VAT.
- Payment instructions and the download licence, in English and German.

**Shop → Shipping prices** — what you charge per zone (Germany / EU / rest of world)
for *Flat* items (prints, cards — charged once per order) and *Parcel* items
(originals, framed — charged per piece).

**Settings → Site settings** — tagline, footer text, contact email, Instagram/Pinterest
links, and the image used when your site is shared.

**Legal pages** (Impressum, Datenschutz, AGB, Shipping & returns) are created as
**drafts with placeholders**. Fill in your real details, **have them checked**
(an online shop in Germany needs correct legal texts), then publish.

## Visitors can change the look, too

The little palette button in the header lets every visitor pick **Light / Dark / Auto**
and one of five colour palettes (Atelier, Meadow, Harbour, Blossom, Dusk). It's
remembered on their device. You don't need to do anything.

---

## If something goes wrong

- *"I made a mistake on a page"* → open the page, **History** shows older versions
  you can restore.
- *"A customer says they paid but nothing happened"* → the site can't see payments by
  itself. Check PayPal/your bank, then set the order to **Paid**.
- *"I can't log in"* → ask for a password reset.
- Anything else: contact your site helper — the backups run every night.
